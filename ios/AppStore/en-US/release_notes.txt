V Bot build 17 matches Grok Bot’s conversation feel on the OpenMaus backend.

• Composer stays above the keyboard instead of hiding under it
• Group chats get Grok-style @mentions so you pick which bot talks
• @mention picker shows each bot’s name and color
• Group typing names the bot that’s working
• Pin or unpin from the chat itself (header long-press, + menu, or profile), with a confirm step
• Confirmation haptic on pin, light haptic when a reply lands, tapback haptic on reactions
• Pinned chats stay on the home strip
• Fixed message sending when the optional desktop-engine status route is unavailable
